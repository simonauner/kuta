02 / THE MOVE

Bring your
whole history.

Strava lets you download everything you have saved as a ZIP file. Upload it to Kuta and your activities are imported in the background. No connection to your Strava account is needed.

  1. On strava.com, go to Settings → My Account → Download Your Account → Get Started.
  2. Click Request Download and wait for the email from Strava.
  3. Upload the ZIP file under Settings → Import in Kuta.

WHAT COMES WITH YOU

  • Your activities with route and measurements from your watch's files.
  • Names, descriptions and the race, long run and commute tags.
  • Shoes and bikes linked to your activities.
  • Heart rate, if you have agreed to it being stored.

Photos, contacts and private notes are not imported. Duplicates are skipped, so you can import again.

03 / TO BE HONEST

Not here yet.

Segments and leaderboards

Kuta has no segments. Groups have a weekly leaderboard for members who have chosen to share their weekly totals.

Routes

You cannot draw routes, get route suggestions or save routes for offline use.

Recording in the app

Kuta does not record activities itself. You upload FIT or GPX files from your watch or log activities manually.

YOUR TRAINING. YOUR DATA.

No paywall. No ads.

Everything Kuta calculates from your own training is free. It is run on the support of voluntary supporters. Activities are private from the start, training data is stored in Sweden and you choose whether heart rate is saved.
